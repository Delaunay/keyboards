Key layout

Drag a cap to slide it along its curve. Shift-drag a cap, or drag the curve line, to move the whole group. Along places the key on the curve, Up lifts it off the curve, and Tilt sets any angle. Arrows nudge it: left and right along the curve, up and down off it. Alt with up or down changes tilt.
